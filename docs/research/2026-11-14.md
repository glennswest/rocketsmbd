## 2. Specs and neighbours
- MS-SMB2 is at revision 87.0 (2026-07-14); 84.0–87.0 were all "major". I could not read
  their change notes: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/openspecs/windows_protocols/ms-smb2/
  Someone should diff the revision history against what we implement (negotiate contexts,
  leases, encryption).
- Linux ksmbd CVEs (kernel in-kernel SMB server, not Samba) are good prior art for our own
  review. Vendor summaries, e.g. https://access.redhat.com/security/cve/CVE-2026-72381 :
  - CVE-2026-72381 durable-handle reconnect use-after-free. We implement no durable handles: not affected.
  - CVE-2026-68431 short SMB2 transform packet over-read. `decrypt_transform`
    (src/smb2/mod.rs:648) rejects frames shorter than the 52-byte header: looks fine; a
    fuzz case for 52..=60-byte transforms would pin it.
  - CVE-2026-64391 / 64393 (ADS I/O and SET_INFO using the current task's credentials
    rather than those from open time) and CVE-2026-68083 (path resolution on create):
    relevant lessons for #53 (per-client identity) and #56 (openat2 RESOLVE_BENEATH).
- No Samba advisory found for November.
- Linux cifs/ksmbd got experimental SMB over QUIC patches (2026-04-28,
  https://yhbt.net/lore/linux-cifs/20260428160020.226512-1-henrique.carvalho@suse.com/);
  not planned for us, noted only.
